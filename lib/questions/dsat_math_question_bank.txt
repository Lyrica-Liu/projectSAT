QUESTION 1 | Algebra | EASY | Multiple Choice
Prompt: If 3x + 5 = 20, what is the value of x?
A) 3
B) 5
C) 8
D) 15

Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Subtracting 5 from both sides gives 3x = 15, and dividing both sides by 3 gives x = 5.
False Answer Explanations:
A) Choice A is incorrect; this would result from dividing 20 by (3+5)=8 incorrectly rather than isolating x properly.
C) Choice C is incorrect; this results from subtracting 3 from 20 instead of 5, then dividing by... this does not follow correct steps and is a common arithmetic slip.
D) Choice D is incorrect; this is the value of 3x, not x itself.
