Six ways to join the 800

Every mark below is one connected “800” with the 8 set larger than the zeros. Each option shows the mark at size, the wordmark lockup, and the app tile at 34px. Tell me the number you want and I'll cut the final wordmark, app mark, and favicon from it.

ChainOne

Three rings on one baseline, tangent to each other — the 8 is a stacked pair, the zeros hang off it like links. Monoline, no type dependency, survives to 16px.

RuleTwo

A single hairline runs the full width and the three counters sit on it, knocking it out where they cross. The most editorial of the set — it reads as a rule in a book, not a logo.

RibbonThree

One continuous ribbon: the 8 unwinds out of its lower bowl and becomes the two zeros without lifting. The most literal 连笔 reading — warmest, least geometric.

LigatureFour

Set, not drawn: Source Serif 4 semibold with the zeros dropped to two-thirds and tracked in until they touch, joined by a short entry stroke. The mark stays in the family's voice.

EclipseFive

The 8 goes solid wine and the zeros stay as ink outlines that overlap it — the only place in the system where wine takes a filled shape. Highest contrast at small sizes.

ColophonSix

A printer's colophon: the 8 at full height with the zeros dropped onto a short wine baseline inside a hairline box — the current app mark, resolved. Quietest, most bookish.
